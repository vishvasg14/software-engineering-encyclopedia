# System Design Books Reference

This file catalogs the books and resources on system design, distributed systems, and software architecture that the System Design document draws from.

## Foundational books

- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* — Martin Kleppmann (O'Reilly). **THE book for distributed systems and data.** Free online at <https://dataintensive.net/>.
- *Site Reliability Engineering* — Betsy Beyer, Chris Jones, Jennifer Petoff, Niall Murphy (O'Reilly). Free online at <https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/>.
- *The Pragmatic Programmer* — David Thomas, Andrew Hunt (Addison-Wesley). Foundational engineering practices.

## Microservices and architecture

- *Building Microservices* — Sam Newman (O'Reilly). The canonical book on microservices design.
- *Microservices Patterns* — Chris Richardson (Manning). Deep dive into patterns.
- *Monolith to Microservices* — Sam Newman (O'Reilly). Migration strategies.
- *Software Architecture: The Hard Parts* — Neal Ford, Pramod Sadalage, Mark Richards (O'Reilly). Trade-off analysis.
- *Fundamentals of Software Architecture* — Mark Richards, Neal Ford (O'Reilly).
- *Clean Architecture* — Robert C. Martin (Prentice Hall).
- *Architecture Patterns with Python* — Bob Gregory, Harry Percival (O'Reilly).

## Domain-Driven Design

- *Domain-Driven Design* — Eric Evans (Addison-Wesley). The original.
- *Domain-Driven Design Distilled* — Vaughn Vernon (Addison-Wesley). Concise overview.
- *Implementing Domain-Driven Design* — Vaughn Vernon (Addison-Wesley). Practical guide.
- *Patterns, Principles, and Practices of Domain-Driven Design* — Scott Millett, Nick Tune (Wrox).

## Event-driven architecture

- *Building Event-Driven Microservices* — Adam Bellemare (O'Reilly). Practical.
- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* — Martin Kleppmann (O'Reilly). Chapter 11 on stream processing.
- *Event Storming* — Alberto Brandolini.

## Reactive systems

- *Reactive Manifesto:* <https://www.reactivemanifesto.org/>
- *Reactive Microservices* — Jonas Bonér.

## Distributed systems theory

- *Distributed Systems* — Maarten van Steen, Andrew Tanenbaum (free online). Classic textbook.
- *Introduction to Reliable and Secure Distributed Systems* — Christian Cachin, Rachid Guerraoui, Luís Rodrigues (Springer).
- *Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events in a Distributed System* — Leslie Lamport (1978). The foundational paper.
- *Paxos Made Simple* — Leslie Lamport (2001).
- *In Search of an Understandable Consensus Algorithm* — Diego Ongaro, John Ousterhout (Raft, 2014).
- *CAP Twelve Years Later* — Eric Brewer (2012). The "CAP" clarification.
- *A Certain Freedom: Rethinking PACELC* — Daniel Abadi (2010).

## System design interviews

- *System Design Interview* — Alex Xu (ByteByteGo). Volume 1 and 2.
- *System Design Interview — An Insider's Guide* — Alex Xu.
- *Designing Data-Intensive Applications* — Martin Kleppmann (frequently cited in interviews).
- *The System Design Primer* — Donne Martin (free on GitHub).
- *Grokking the System Design Interview* — Design Gurus.
- *Hello Interview* — free online system design practice.

## Architecture decision records

- *Building Evolutionary Architectures* — Neal Ford, Rebecca Parsons, Patrick Kua (O'Reilly).
- *Documenting Software Architectures* — Clements et al. (Addison-Wesley).
- *Architecture Decision Records:** <https://adr.github.io/> (lightweight ADR format).

## Cloud-native architecture

- *Cloud Native Patterns* — Cornelia Davis (Manning).
- *Kubernetes Patterns* — Bilgin Ibryam, Roland Huß (O'Reilly). Free online.
- *Designing Distributed Systems* — Brendan Burns (O'Reilly). Free online.
- *Production-Ready Microservices* — Susan Fowler (O'Reilly). Free online.

## Performance and observability

- *Systems Performance* — Brendan Gregg (Prentice Hall). The canonical book.
- *The Site Reliability Workbook* — Betsy Beyer et al. (O'Reilly). Free online.
- *Observability Engineering* — Charity Majors, Lorin Hochstein, George Miranda (O'Reilly).
